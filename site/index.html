Keyboard shortcuts and what the numbers mean
  • Space play / pause   ← → one piece (Shift: 10 pieces)   Home / End start / end
  • N / P next / previous line clear   + / - faster / slower
  • Height: tallest column (20 = top of the visible board; 21–22 = the two dimmed spawn rows, about to lose). Holes: empty squares with a block above them. Bumpiness: how uneven the surface is (sum of height differences between neighbouring columns).
  • White rows = the moment before they clear (shown at speeds up to 20 pieces/s). The outlined piece is the one just placed.
